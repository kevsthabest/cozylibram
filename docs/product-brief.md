# Cozy Libram: product brief

Cozy Libram is a BookTok-inspired book tracking app. Its primary use is to track
the reading habits of its users: what they read, how much, and how it felt.

What makes it different is that it also helps readers understand books, not just
log them. Three layers sit on top of the tracker:
- Tropes help readers discover their next book (enemies-to-lovers, forced
  proximity, morally grey).
- Characters help readers live in a book after loving it (cast, relationships,
  favourites).
- Content warnings help readers trust the app with their choices (clear,
  spoiler-safe, reader-controlled).
Readers contribute what they notice, so every reader makes the app better for the
next one.

## Who it's for
Passionate readers with large libraries and long TBRs, who think in tropes,
check warnings before starting, and love talking about books.

## How it should feel
Cozy: pick it up and feel at home. It celebrates what has happened and never
pressures what comes next. There are no reading goals, challenges or timers.
It's private by default; social features are opt-in.

Play is welcome between friends: seeing what your circle is reading,
celebrating each other's months, playful superlatives. It stays cozy under
three conditions — opt-in, unranked, and celebrating rather than pressuring.
No ordinal ranks, no sorted-by-output boards, no one reader's pace held up
against another's. Awareness of friends is warmth; ranking them is pressure.

## Tests for any new feature
1. Does it make reading feel like a chore?
2. Would a reader feel watched, scored or compared?
3. Does it help someone understand a book, or only count it?
4. Does it ask the reader to do work the app could do itself?

## Voice
Warm, brief and kind. Playful, never pressuring. Never guilt, never "software"
language. Places, not features: The Library, The Hearth, My Shelves.
