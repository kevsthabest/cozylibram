# Cozy Libram Glossary

## Naming convention

Cozy Libram's user-facing language follows one rule: **every section is a place in a cozy reading house**, not a thing you operate. The app's goal is "Cozy, BookTok inspired" — warm, literary, slightly magical — so names sound like rooms you'd walk into, never like software features.

What this rules out: tech words (hub, dashboard, feed, portal), corporate words (network, community as a label), and anything that needs explaining. What this rules in: hearths, libraries, shelves, nooks, stacks — the vocabulary the themes already speak (Hearthside, Candlelight, Harvest, Yuletide).

Functional actions (Add a Book, Settings) stay plain on purpose — they're verbs, not places, and dressing them up would only confuse.

## Terms

## The Library
The reader's home in the app: their collection, currently-reading books, and seasonal greeting. The heart of the house — everything else is a room off of it.

## My Shelves
The 3D shelf view of the reader's books. "Shelves," never "collections" — a shelf is furniture you can picture; a collection is a database word.

## The Hearth
The social section: friends, reading circles, recommendations, buddy reads. People gather around a hearth. Replaces the old theme-specific names (Harvest Court, Night Court) and the internal "coven" — one name, every theme, matching the private-by-default / social-by-choice direction.

## Chronicles
The stats section: the story of the reader's reading life — pages, streaks, milestones — told like a chronicle, not a report. Renamed from "Stats" (too clinical) in v486. Never "dashboard."

## Wander the Stacks *(proposed)*
The discover section. Browsing a library's stacks is wandering with intent; "discover" is what an algorithm does to you. Not yet implemented; "Discover" is the current label.
